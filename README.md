## 👋 About Me

I'm **Zaiyan Zhang (张再筵)**, an M.E. student in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing at Wuhan University and a Research Intern at Shanghai AI Laboratory. My research focuses on **remote sensing image processing**, **multimodal foundation models**, and **AI agents**.

## 🔍 Research Code

- **[ECRformer](https://github.com/zzaiyan/ECRformer)** · ISPRS 2026 — Efficient cloud removal for multimodal satellite imagery with semantic-decoupled learning.
- **[MS2TAN](https://github.com/CUG-BEODL/MS2TAN)** · IEEE TGRS 2025 — Optical time-series image reconstruction with masked spatial-temporal attention.
- **[CoRE-UIR](https://github.com/zzaiyan/CoRE-UIR)** · Under Review — Efficient all-in-one remote sensing image restoration with common and residual experts.
- **[PriorCR](https://github.com/zzaiyan/PriorCR)** · Under Review — Physics-informed optical-SAR fusion for robust cloud removal.
- **[GeoSeg](https://github.com/zzaiyan/GeoSeg)** · Under Review — Open-vocabulary remote sensing segmentation with geospatial structural priors.
